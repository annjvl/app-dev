# 🎬 My Favorite Series

## 🌟 FROM

**FROM** is a *horror* and *mystery* series that follows a group of people trapped in a strange town where leaving is impossible.

---

## 📖 Overview
The story is about:
- A mysterious town that traps anyone who enters  
- People trying to survive from dangerous creatures at night  
- Finding clues to escape the place  

---

## ❤️ Why I Love This Series
- 😱 Very suspenseful and creepy  
- 🧠 Makes you think and question everything  
- 🔥 Unique and unpredictable story  
- 🎭 Strong and emotional characters  

---

## 🎭 Main Characters

| Character | Description |
|----------|------------|
| Boyd Stevens | The town's leader trying to keep everyone safe |
| Tabitha Matthews | A mother searching for answers |
| Jim Matthews | A father determined to escape |
| Ethan Matthews | A child who sees things differently |

---
